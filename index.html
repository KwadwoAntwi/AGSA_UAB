Who We Are

Rooted in Culture. Rising in Excellence.

AGSA exists to support African graduate students at UAB by creating a welcoming community where culture, scholarship, leadership, and belonging can grow together.

01

Community

We create spaces where students can connect, build friendships, share experiences, and find support.

02

Culture

We celebrate African identity through food, music, fashion, language, storytelling, and shared traditions.

03

Excellence

We recognize the academic, research, professional, and leadership journey of African graduate students.
