Who We Are

About AGSA

The African Graduate Students Association is dedicated to creating a welcoming, supportive, and culturally rich community for African graduate students at UAB.

We organize social events, academic support opportunities, cultural celebrations, collaborations, and community-building activities that help students connect, grow, and feel seen.

Community

Creating spaces where students can connect, relax, and belong.

Culture

Celebrating African identity, stories, food, music, and traditions.

Support

Helping students navigate graduate school, life, and leadership.
